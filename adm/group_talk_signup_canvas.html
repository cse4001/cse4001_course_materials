This is the sign-up for the group talk ( 15% of the overall grade). It is a group assignment: one member submits on behalf of the whole team. Do not submit separately.

Join your team under People → CSE4001 Group Talks first. Teams are 4 students. Due Thu Sep 10 (Week 4). The first talk is Thu Sep 24.

Topics and dates

The topic and the date are fixed. Teams choose the slot and, more importantly, the second operating system. The second system in the table below is a suggestion. Propose a different one if you want.

The eight slots

Talk Date Topic Suggested comparison
1 Thu Sep 24
Week 6		 How a process is created Unix fork+exec with copy-on-write, versus Windows CreateProcess as a single call.
2 Thu Oct 1
Week 7		 Message passing Linux pipes and System V / POSIX queues, versus Mach ports in macOS. Byte streams versus typed messages with capabilities.
3 Thu Oct 8
Week 8		 Isolation without a VM Linux namespaces and cgroups (what Docker actually does), versus FreeBSD jails — which came first and took a simpler path.
4 Thu Oct 15
Week 9		 How a mutex really works Linux futexes and the uncontended fast path, versus Windows SRW locks and keyed events.
5 Thu Oct 22
Week 10		 Avoiding locks entirely Linux RCU, versus epoch-based reclamation in FreeBSD or hazard pointers.
6 Thu Nov 5
Week 12		 Kernel memory allocation Linux buddy allocator plus slab, versus FreeBSD's UMA zone allocator. Both solve fragmentation; they disagree about how.
7 Thu Nov 12
Week 13		 Sharing a device that is itself a computer GPU scheduling and memory under Linux (DRM, the vendor driver, MIG and MPS) versus Windows WDDM, which schedules and preempts the GPU from the OS.
8 Thu Nov 19
Week 14		 Surviving a crash ext4 journaling, versus copy-on-write in ZFS or btrfs. Two answers to the same consistency problem.
